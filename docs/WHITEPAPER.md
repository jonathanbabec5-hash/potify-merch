# POTIFY Merch Directory
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Store integration reference**

### Goal and audience

Connect the book, film and games to a real merchandise destination. The immediate audience is fans seeking a physical piece of the project.

### Design and implementation

A small public catalog records the established store and product link from the First Cycle edition. Checkout and fulfillment belong to the merchant platform. Leave checkout, fulfillment and refund handling on the merchant platform. Public referral links should identify the campaign without storing buyer details.

### What a contributor can establish

The public entry page returned HTTP 200 and rendered local content in Chromium at a 390 × 844 viewport. Link health, product availability and fulfilled orders are independent checks. Historical product URLs may need new inventory evidence before posting.

### Evidence and outstanding work

Product stock, pricing, order export and current conversion are unknown. The reference never processes payment. The next acceptance case is concrete. From a sample referral, open the intended listing and reconcile a consenting test order with the platform if available.

### Place in POTIFY

Target one fulfilled sale. Report net after production, fees, shipping subsidy and refunds. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
