# POTIFY Merch Directory marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Fans seeking a physical piece of the project. **Offer.** Connect the book, film and games to a real merchandise destination.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show one physical item in context and publish only currently confirmed product details. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Visit the official storefront. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 10 qualified product views. |

### Sales test

Start with the POTIFY tote already linked from Book I. Target one fulfilled order and calculate net from the provider receipt, unit cost, platform fees and refunds. Target one fulfilled sale. Report net after production, fees, shipping subsidy and refunds.

### Measurement and decision

Use `merch-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Product stock, pricing, order export and current conversion are unknown. The reference never processes payment. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
